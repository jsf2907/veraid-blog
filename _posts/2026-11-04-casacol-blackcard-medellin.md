## How does VeraID work?

1. **Ask for their TRU ID.** Guides, drivers, hosts and professionals on VeraID each have one permanent TRU ID.
2. **Look it up in the app.** You see their photo, first name and verification status in seconds.
3. **Log a check-in.** Keep a record of who you met and when, right in your history.

Every provider on VeraID verifies their identity with an official ID document and a biometric face scan, followed by a human review before their TRU ID is issued. As a visitor, you never upload your own ID. You only look up the person you're about to meet.
