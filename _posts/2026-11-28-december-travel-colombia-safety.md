## Key takeaways

- December is Colombia's big celebration month: Medellín's Christmas lights, the Feria de Cali and family parties fill streets, flights and rentals.
- Book stays, tours and drivers early, in writing, and never pay by wire, gift card, crypto or payment app only.
- Crowds mean pickpockets. Carry little, keep your phone close and leave jewelry and watches at home.
- Use app or dispatched taxis, not street taxis, and plan your ride home before a late night.
- Keep your distance from street fireworks, watch your drink, and call 123 in an emergency.

</div>

December in Colombia is a wonderful time to visit, and staying safe mostly comes down to planning ahead for crowds. Book your stay, tours and rides early through traceable channels, carry less in busy places, use app or dispatched taxis, and keep new acquaintances in public places. Do that and you can enjoy the lights, music and food without the usual holiday-season headaches.

## Why is December such a busy month to travel in Colombia?

December is when Colombia celebrates. Cities light up, families travel to see relatives, and festivals fill the calendar from early December into January. For visitors that means great atmosphere, but also fuller flights, higher prices, and more people in the same streets and plazas.

Two events draw especially big crowds:

- **Medellín's Christmas lights (alumbrados).** The 2026 lights were presented on November 20, with a theme honoring the regions of Colombia, and run from December to mid-January. Visiting is free ([Semana, November 18, 2026](https://www.semana.com/turismo/articulo/alumbrados-de-medellin-2026-anuncian-que-ya-esta-listo-el-montaje-y-la-fecha-oficial-del-encendido/202610/)).
- **The Feria de Cali.** In 2025 it ran from December 25 to 30 and opened with the Salsódromo parade on December 25 ([Alcaldía de Cali](https://www.cali.gov.co/publicaciones/190506/la-sucursal-del-cielo-se-adorna-para-su-fiesta-mas-popular-la-edicion-68-de-la-feria-de-cali/)). Check the city's official program for the 2026 dates.

None of this makes December unsafe. It simply changes the risks: more crowds, more last-minute bookings and more late nights.

## How do you avoid booking scams during the holidays?

High demand is exactly when fake listings and fake tour offers appear. A place that looks perfect, cheap and "available only if you pay today" is the classic setup.

The U.S. Federal Trade Commission lists clear warning signs. If someone insists you pay by wire transfer, gift card, cryptocurrency or a payment app only, treat it as a scam. Search the company or host name together with words like "scam", "review" or "complaint", and get the cancellation policy before you pay ([FTC, avoid scams when you travel](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/avoid-scams-when-you-travel)). For rentals, the FTC also says to walk away from anyone who pressures you to decide quickly, and to get a copy of the contract before paying any deposit ([FTC, rental listing scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/rental-listing-scams)).

A few habits help:

- Book stays and tours early, while you still have choices and time to check.
- Keep payments and messages on the booking platform when you use one.
- Get the price, dates, pickup point and cancellation terms in writing.
- Pay by card where you can, so there's a record.

## How do you stay safe in big holiday crowds?

Plazas, light displays, parades and concerts are where pickpockets work best. The U.S. State Department notes that criminals pick out tourists by things like visible jewelry and watches ([U.S. State Department, Colombia country information](https://travel.state.gov/content/passports/en/country/colombia.html)).

Simple steps go a long way:

- Carry one card and a little cash. Leave your passport in the safe and carry a copy.
- Keep your phone in a front pocket or a zipped bag, and step aside before using it.
- Agree on a meeting point with your group in case you get separated.
- Arrive earlier than the biggest rush and leave a little before the end.

For Medellín's lights, local coverage recommends planning your route ahead, checking official EPM and city channels, and using public transport, especially the Metro, when you can ([IFM Noticias, November 20, 2026](https://ifmnoticias.com/gente-bacana/medellin-enciende-la-navidad-los-alumbrados-iluminan-las-tradiciones-de-colombia/)). In the 2025 season the lights were on daily from 6 p.m. to midnight, with special hours on December 24 and 31, along Parques del Río and Avenida La Playa among other spots ([Alcaldía de Medellín, November 2025](https://www.medellin.gov.co/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/con-ocho-millones-de-bombillas-led-epm-encendio-los-alumbrados-en-navidad-medellin-te-quiere/)). Check this year's hours and locations before you go.

## How should you get around on late holiday nights?

Getting home is often the riskiest part of a night out. The State Department advises against hailing taxis on the street and recommends a dispatch service or a phone app whenever possible ([U.S. State Department](https://travel.state.gov/content/passports/en/country/colombia.html)). Canada's travel advice adds: never get into a taxi that already has a passenger, and avoid cars without plates ([Government of Canada, Colombia travel advice](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/colombia)).

Before you head out:

- Decide how you'll get home before the first drink.
- Book through an app or have your hotel or host call a taxi.
- Check that the plate and car match what the app shows.
- At Bogotá's El Dorado airport, use the authorized taxi line outside the terminal and check the plate with the airport's tool ([El Dorado airport, taxis](https://eldorado.aero/en/servicios/taxis)).

If you hire a private driver for a holiday day trip, confirm the booking in writing and know exactly who is coming to pick you up.

## What about fireworks?

Fireworks (pólvora) are part of many December celebrations, and they hurt people every year. Colombia's Instituto Nacional de Salud had counted 505 people burned or injured by fireworks in December 2024 by around December 21, 185 of them minors ([RTVC Noticias, December 22, 2024](https://www.rtvcnoticias.com/actualidad/cifras-personas-quemadas-polvora-diciembre-2024)).

As a visitor, the safest approach is simple: enjoy fireworks from a distance, don't handle them, and keep children well back from anyone lighting them in the street.

## How do you meet new people safely during the holidays?

December is social. You'll meet other travelers, locals at parties, and maybe matches on dating apps. Most of these meetings are friendly, and a few habits keep them that way.

The UK government warns that criminals in Colombia have used drugs, including scopolamine, to rob people, often through food, drinks or cigarettes, and that some use dating apps to lure foreigners traveling alone. It advises not leaving food or drinks unattended, not taking anything from strangers, and not inviting new acquaintances back to your accommodation ([UK FCDO, Colombia safety and security](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/colombia/safety-and-security)). It also notes that drinking alcohol in public spaces is illegal.

For anyone you meet online, a short video call before meeting is a good first step. It's a good sign, not a guarantee. Then meet in a busy public place and tell a friend where you'll be.

## What should you do if something goes wrong?

- **In an emergency in Colombia, call 123.**
- **File a police report online** through the Policía Nacional's ADenunciar service ([Policía Nacional](https://www.policia.gov.co/noticia/una-herramienta-al-servicio-ciudadania-adenunciar)).
- **Call your bank** right away if a card is stolen or used.
- **U.S. citizens** can find embassy contact details on the State Department's Colombia page ([U.S. State Department](https://travel.state.gov/content/passports/en/country/colombia.html)).

This is general information, not legal advice.

## FAQ

### Is December a good time to visit Colombia?

Yes, for many travelers it's the most festive time of year. Expect crowds, higher prices and busy flights, so book early and plan your transport.

### When are Medellín's Christmas lights on?

The 2026 lights run from December to mid-January and are free to visit. Last season they were on from 6 p.m. to midnight daily, with special hours on December 24 and 31. Check the official EPM and city channels for this year's hours.

### When is the Feria de Cali?

In 2025 it ran from December 25 to 30, opening with the Salsódromo parade. Check the Alcaldía de Cali's program for 2026 dates.

### How can I tell if a holiday rental or tour is a scam?

Watch for pressure to pay fast, prices far below similar options, and requests for wire, gift card, crypto or payment-app-only payments. Get everything in writing and pay with a traceable method.

### Can I use VeraID to check a holiday tour guide or driver?

Yes, if they have a TRU ID. Look it up before you meet to see their photo, first name and verification status. If they don't have one, use the booking checks in this guide.
